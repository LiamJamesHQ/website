What your deposit does

A place held, while you decide

Your £500 commitment deposit secures your position in the build queue and is deducted in full from your final invoice.

01The £500 deposit is not an additional charge. It is deducted in full from your final invoice.
02It reserves your place in the queue for 72 hours while the final details of your sauna are confirmed.
03No materials are ordered and no production begins until your final invoice is paid in full.
04Once your specification is agreed, we issue your final invoice.

If the invoice is not settled within 72 hours your place may be released. The £500 deposit is non-refundable.

The process

What happens next

From the moment your deposit lands to the day your sauna arrives, here is how your build progresses.

01Your £500 commitment deposit is received and your place in the queue is reserved for 72 hours.
02A member of our team will contact you to confirm your final sauna specification and answer any remaining questions.
03We issue your final invoice based on the agreed specification.
04Once the final invoice has been paid in full, your order is approved for production.
05Materials are ordered and your project is scheduled for production.
06We send your job intake form to capture any final project information.
07Your dedicated contact remains with you through to delivery and sign off.
